Our Story

Hana Café: A Haven Born Out of Love for Exceptional Coffee & Sweet Moments

Founded in the heart of Nairobi, Hana Café (inspired by "Hana" - the Japanese word for flower) was created as a gentle, elegant space where friends gather, ideas blossom, and every sip feels like a warm hug.

We blend premium single-origin Kenyan Arabica beans from local highlands with handcrafted organic honey syrups and French-inspired baking techniques. Whether you're working on your laptop, taking photos with our blossom wall, or sharing a cheesecake slice with loved ones, Hana is your cozy home away from home.
